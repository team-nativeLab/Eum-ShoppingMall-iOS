## High-fidelity component notes

- `HF/Icon` (`174:315`): Lucide 0.460, 1.5 pt stroke, text-1 color; sizes 16/20/24/28. Emphasized state uses 2 pt stroke.
- `HF/HeroBanner` (`177:569`): 350 × 208 pt, 12 pt radius, ph-3. Autoplay every 5 seconds; pause on touch; disabled under Reduce Motion.
- `HF/ProductCard` (`176:493`): Grid 169 pt / Carousel 148 pt. Hidden-price state says “구매 불가” and hides price; sold-out overlays white at 60% with a sold-out badge.
- `HF/Button` (`175:123`): L56 / M48 / S36, 8 pt radius; default/pressed/disabled; web hover resembles pressed; focus ring 2 pt. UIKit/SwiftUI visual states still require implementation validation.
- `HF/SearchBar` (`175:276`): Home instance 350 × 44 pt; 16 pt horizontal inset, 8 pt icon/text gap, 20 pt search icon, surface fill, capsule radius. Implemented as SwiftUI TextField with an extracted Figma SVG.
- `HF/TextField` (`175:345`): label gap 8 pt; field height 48 pt, 14 pt horizontal inset, 8 pt radius. The selected size-field context shows a 1 pt control border; error/focus variants still need their own node audit.
- `HF/CategoryItem` (home instance `178:426`): 76 × 84 pt; 24 pt icon; 6 pt gap; 4 pt horizontal/8 pt vertical inset; 12 pt radius.
- `HF/App/TopBar` (home instance `178:380`): 390 × 56 pt; horizontal inset 8 pt; 44 pt menu and notification targets with 24 pt icons.
- `HF/App/TabBar` (`178:679`): 390 × 91 pt; five 78 pt items, 24 pt icons, 11 pt labels, 56 pt item region, and a 34 pt bottom-indicator region.
- Home section header (`178:536`): 350 × 26 pt; title and optional “더보기” action with a 16 pt chevron.
- `HF/EmptyState` (`177:455`, A-06z instance `180:1715`): 64 pt surface icon tile with 28 pt search-x icon; 64/32 pt vertical padding; 160 × 48 pt action; state copy is node-specific.
- `HF/CartItem` (A-07 instance `177:258`): 138 pt row; 20 pt selection control, 80 pt placeholder image, product title/option, price, and 112 × 36 pt quantity stepper; sold-out row disables selection and quantity.
- `HF/Checkbox` (A-07 instances `175:182` and `175:187`): 20 pt square, 4 pt radius; selected uses text-1 fill and inverse check, disabled uses surface-2 fill.
- `HF/QuantityStepper` (A-07 instances `175:258` and `175:269`): S size is 112 × 36 pt with 36/40/36 pt minus/value/plus segments and 8 pt radius; min and disabled controls use disabled text color.
- A-07 also uses a 44 pt inline login notice, 12 pt radius price summary, fixed 56 pt primary order button, and the five-item tab bar with selected cart state.
- A-07h (`181:1738`) shows two selected priced items and one disabled quote item. The unavailable-items group header is 16 pt with 24/12 pt top/bottom padding; a two-line 14 pt note explains quote/sold-out exclusion. The quote row has an outlined `견적 상품` badge, `견적 문의 →` action, and `가격 문의` label.
- A-07h2 (`181:1940`) shows zero orderable items and two disabled quote rows. The selection count is `0/0`, the price summary is `₩0`, and the 56 pt bottom CTA changes to `담은 상품으로 견적 문의 (2)`.
- A-08 (`181:2106`) uses a three-step checkout progress indicator, 44 pt section headers, a 48 pt delivery request field, order-item collapse control, and fixed 56 pt `다음` CTA. Its initial frame shows the default address, standard delivery, two order rows, and a sample expected total.
- A-12 (`182:2416`) shows a 320 pt left drawer over a 48% dimmed home screen. It includes a 44 pt close row, brand/member summary, five 52 pt navigation rows, and a bottom settings/logout footer above the 34 pt home-indicator area.
- A-13 (`182:2524`) uses a modal login top bar, 26 pt brand heading, inline login notice, two 48 pt fields, keep-signed-in checkbox, disabled 56 pt login action, account links, and two 48 pt social login actions.
